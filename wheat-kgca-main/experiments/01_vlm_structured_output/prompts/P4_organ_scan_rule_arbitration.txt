Allowed wheat stages:

Stage definitions:
1. seedling / BBCH 10-19: low wheat plants with unfolded leaves; no obvious tillering cluster, no internode elongation, and no visible spike.
2. tillering / BBCH 20-29: multiple basal tillers and clustered shoots; no clear internode elongation and no visible spike.
3. stem_elongation / BBCH 30-36: upright and taller culms with internode elongation; the spike is not visible.
4. flag_leaf_booting / BBCH 37-49: flag leaf is visible or the leaf sheath is swollen; the spike is still hidden in the boot or only barely indicated.
5. heading / BBCH 51-59: spike tip to full spike has emerged; the spike is clearly visible, but anthers are not clearly visible.
6. flowering / BBCH 61-69: anthers are visible on the spike.
7. grain_filling / BBCH 71-77: spike is filled and grain development is visible; plants are still green or mixed yellow-green.
8. maturity / BBCH 83-89: plants are clearly yellowing or dry; spike and grains are mature.

First scan visible agronomic evidence, then classify, then run an agronomic consistency check.

R4. If spike is full_grain and canopy is green or mixed_yellow, grain_filling should be considered.
